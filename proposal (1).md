## In just a sentence or two, summarize your project. (E.g., "A website that lets you buy and sell stocks.")

My project will be a game Breakout, where the player controls a paddle to bounce a ball and break bricks. The goal is to clear all the bricks without letting the ball fall past the paddle.

## In a paragraph or more, detail your project. What will your software do? What features will it have? How will it be executed?

The player will move a paddle left and right at the bottom of the screen to bounce a ball upward toward a wall of bricks. When the ball hits a brick, the brick will disappear, and the player will earn points. If the player misses the ball and it falls below the paddle, they lose a life. The game will feature multiple levels with increasing difficulty and their score will be tracked. I also may add power-ups, such as making the paddle bigger or multiplying the ball, and a start menu and game-over screen.
